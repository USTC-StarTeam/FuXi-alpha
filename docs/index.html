1. Paper

Yufei Ye, Wei Guo, Jin Yao Chin, Hao Wang, Hong Zhu, Xi Lin, Yuyang Ye, Yong Liu, Ruiming Tang, Defu Lian, and Enhong Chen. FuXi-alpha: Scaling Recommendation Model with Feature Interaction Enhanced Transformer. In Companion Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2025, pages 557-566, 2025.

FuXi-alpha studies how to scale sequential recommendation with explicit temporal, positional, and semantic interaction channels.
